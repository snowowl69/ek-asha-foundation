Why Donate to Ek Asha Foundation?

Your donation directly supports education programs for underprivileged children, healthcare camps in rural areas, women empowerment initiatives, environmental conservation, and skill development training across Haryana, Delhi, and Chandigarh.

We are a registered non-government trust (Registration No. 2168) and operate with full transparency. Every contribution is accounted for and used towards creating meaningful, lasting change.

गुप्त दान (Anonymous Donation)

In Indian tradition, Gupt Daan (गुप्त दान) is considered the highest form of charity — giving selflessly without seeking recognition. We deeply respect this tradition.

You can donate completely anonymously — no name, no contact details required. Simply toggle the "Gupt Daan" option in the donation form, enter your amount, and contribute with a pure heart. Your identity will remain private.

"दान वो है जो चुपचाप किया जाए — जहाँ बाएं हाथ को पता न चले कि दाएं हाथ ने क्या दिया।"

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Please contact us at ekashafoundation.adm@gmail.com or call +91 92150 00448 for the latest information on tax exemption certificates and eligibility.

How is my donation used?

100% of your donation goes towards our programs — education, healthcare, women's empowerment, vocational training, environment conservation, and community development. We share detailed utilization reports with donors.

Can I donate in kind (goods/materials)?

Absolutely! We accept books, clothes, food supplies, medical equipment, and more. Contact us to arrange a pickup or drop-off at our Kaithal office.

Can I volunteer instead of donating money?

Yes! We welcome volunteers with open arms. Whether you can give time, skills, or expertise — every contribution matters. Reach out via our contact page.

Will I receive a receipt?

Yes. We provide donation receipts for all contributions. For bank transfers and UPI payments, share the transaction details with us and we'll issue a receipt promptly.
