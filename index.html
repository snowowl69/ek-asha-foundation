About Ek Asha Foundation

Established on 26th December 2022, Ek Asha Foundation is a registered non-government trust (NGO ID: HR/2023/0335273) based in Kaithal, Haryana. We believe that every individual deserves access to quality education, healthcare, and opportunities for growth.

Our foundation operates across 3 states — Haryana, Delhi, and Chandigarh — covering more than 15 districts. We work to uplift the underserved, protect the environment, and empower women and youth.

What Drives Us

Our Mission

To create a sustainable impact by providing education, healthcare, vocational training, and social empowerment to marginalized communities. We strive to be the bridge between privilege and need.

Our Vision

A world where every individual — regardless of caste, creed, gender, or economic status — has access to the resources and opportunities they need to lead a dignified and fulfilling life.

Our Values

Compassion, transparency, inclusivity, and sustainability guide every decision we make. We believe in accountable service and measurable impact.

Our Key Causes

We work across multiple sectors to create holistic & lasting change in communities.

Education & Literacy

Running literacy programs, scholarship drives, and after-school coaching for underprivileged children and adults.

Health & Family Welfare

Organizing health camps, awareness drives, and facilitating access to medical services in rural and semi-urban areas.

Women's Empowerment

Supporting women through skill training, self-help groups, legal aid, and entrepreneurship programs.

Environment & Forests

Plantation drives, water conservation projects, and awareness campaigns for sustainable living practices.

Skill Development

Vocational training in tailoring, computers, agriculture, and trades for youth and women to ensure self-reliance.

Child Welfare

Nutrition programs, child protection initiatives, and recreational activities for holistic child development.
